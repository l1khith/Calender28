# Privacy Policy & Terms of Service for Calender28

**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026  

Calender28 ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a decentralized, on-device Eisenhower matrix scheduling and task management platform. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Calender28 mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Storage & Processing (100% On-Device)
- **Local Storage:** All personal tasks, matrix quadrant categorizations, custom tags, habit cycles, and notes created in Calender28 are stored exclusively on your device's local database (Room/SQLite) and Jetpack DataStore.
- **Zero Cloud Tracking:** We do not operate external cloud servers or databases for user synchronization. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal tasks, schedule entries, or user identifiers.

---

### 2. Device Permissions & System Access
Calender28 requests access to specific device capabilities solely to deliver core productivity functionality:
- **Device Calendar Access (`READ_CALENDAR`, `WRITE_CALENDAR`):** Used strictly to import events from your device's calendar into your matrix view and sync schedule changes locally upon your request.
- **Exact Alarms & Notifications (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used exclusively to schedule and fire local task reminders and recurring habit notifications.
- **Storage / File Access (Storage Access Framework):** Used to let you select an external `.ics` file for calendar import or export your matrix backups to `.csv`/`.json` format.
- **Biometric Hardware (`USE_BIOMETRIC`):** Used locally by Android's BiometricPrompt to lock and unlock the app. Biometric data is evaluated entirely by your device hardware and is never accessible to the app.

---

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Calender28 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertisements for Free-tier users. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), device telemetry, and general location data in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.
- **Ad Removal:** Upgrading to the Pro tier removes all third-party advertisement requests and disables ad units within the app.

---

### 4. In-App Purchases & Monetization
- All purchases or subscription entitlements are processed securely through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not store or process your credit card, bank account, or payment credentials.
